# Recording and submission checklist

Use one browser profile and the same website address throughout. Merchant and
resident are demo roles sharing this browser's saved data, not separate accounts.

## Record the complete flow

1. Open **Merchant** and click **Reset demo**. Chocolate Bun stock is **18**,
   original price **RM12**, with no offers or reservations.
2. Save stock **10**, then open **Predictions**: expect **3** likely unsold.
   Recommendations should suggest **3** items at **30% off**. This proves the
   output responds to inventory. Return to Merchant and reset again.
3. Open **Predictions**: stock **18**, expected normal sales **7**, likely unsold
   **11**. The demo clock is fixed at **7:30 PM**, closing **9 PM**.
4. Open **Recommendations**: **11** items, **50% off**, **RM6** each, suggested
   launch **8 PM**. Estimated revenue is a forecast, not money already recovered.
5. Click **Publish rescue offer**. Repeating publication must not create a
   duplicate. Physical stock remains **18**.
6. Open **Resident**, reserve **2** buns. Availability becomes **9**; the
   reservation appears. Refresh to confirm it persists.
7. Open **Merchant**: the reservation appears and physical stock is still **18**.
   Confirm pickup once: status becomes **collected**, stock becomes **16**,
   offer availability remains **9**. A second confirmation must not change stock.
8. Open **Impact**: **2 items rescued**, **RM12.00 recovered**, **0 awaiting
   pickup**. Refresh and verify the same totals.

## Before sending

- Run the production build and the project's automated checks on the final branch.
- Open the deployed address and repeat the flow there before recording. Local
  browser data does not transfer to the deployed website.
- Test the main screens on a narrow/mobile viewport; text and buttons must remain usable.
- Prepare the required **pitch deck, maximum 10 main slides**. An appendix is allowed.
- Provide a **five-minute video or live demo link** demonstrating the solution.
- Provide the required **prototype, codebase, or GitHub repository link**.
- Supporting documents are optional. Include additional items requested by the portal.
- Have every teammate review their contribution and the AI assistance disclosure.
- Complete the real team name, leader, university, member details, email, and
  contact number. Do not leave placeholders in submitted materials.
- Confirm dates and upload requirements on the official portal. The supplied
  template gives **11:59 PM, 23 October 2026**; current portal instructions take priority.

## Ten-slide pitch map

1. Title and real team information
2. Problem
3. Target users
4. Solution
5. Innovation and current forecasting approach
6. How it works, with prototype screenshots
7. Impact, separating demo outcomes from pilot goals
8. Feasibility and limitations
9. Implementation and proposed pilot
10. Why the team should win, **100 words or fewer**

Put supporting details and AI assistance disclosure in the appropriate slide or
appendix. This covers the template's eight main areas and additional prompts.
